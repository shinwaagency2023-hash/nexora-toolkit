03 / Price with purpose

Service pricing worksheet

Build a price from direct product costs, staff time, commission, overhead, and your target margin.

Suggested price = estimated total cost ÷ (1 − target profit margin). This is an illustrative planning estimate, not a guaranteed net margin. Check market positioning, demand, and your real costs before publishing prices.

A considered pricing strategy

Start with your costs, then assess competitor positioning and the value of your expertise. Packages may encourage repeat visits; promotions should still cover costs. Review prices whenever product, labour, or overhead assumptions change.

The discount effect

Illustration: a service priced at 500 with a total estimated cost of 300 leaves 200 before a discount. A 10% discount reduces the selling price to 450 and the difference to 150. The example uses currency-neutral numbers; it does not predict your results.
