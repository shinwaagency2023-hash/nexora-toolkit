05 / Price with intention

Service pricing worksheet

Enter costs for each service and the margin you want to model. Suggested price = total estimated cost ÷ (1 − desired margin ÷ 100). Transfer your labour and supplies estimates where applicable; they are not added automatically. For a recurring contract, enter costs for the same billing period you intend to price.

Margin is not markup

Illustrative example, in any currency: a cost of 100 with a 25% markup sells for 125, giving a 20% margin. For a 25% margin, the selling price is 100 ÷ 0.75 = 133.33 (rounded). These are examples, not business performance figures.
